## Voice

- **EN** — international business English, British spelling (optimise, colour), short sentences.
- **DE** — formal “Sie”, natural German for DACH business readers; avoid anglicisms where a common
  German term exists, keep established ones (SEO, Content-Marketing, Landingpage).
- **IT** — professional, warm, direct; address the reader with “Lei” forms or impersonal phrasing,
  never “tu” in body copy.
- **SQ** — standard Albanian, formal “ju”; correct use of ë and ç; established English terms
  (SEO, CMS, landing page) may stay, explained once if needed.

## Never

- Invent statistics, clients, awards or testimonials in any language.
- Promise rankings, “100% security”, guaranteed sales or ROI.
