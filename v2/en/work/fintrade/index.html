Fintrade

A bilingual website for a procurement company supplying infrastructure, power, water and machinery solutions in Albania and Kosovo.

Overview

Fintrade is a procurement company in Tirana and part of Finman Group. It supplies products and solutions for civil works and infrastructure, machinery and equipment, power, and water and wastewater treatment to clients in Albania and Kosovo.

Challenge

Procurement is a business of trust and range. Engineers and buyers need to see quickly which industries Fintrade serves, what it has delivered and whom to contact — in Albanian or English.

Approach

The site is organised by the industries Fintrade serves, backed by a projects section and a clear company story. A recruitment message in the top bar supports hiring alongside the commercial content.

Design

A light, architectural look in Fintrade's indigo and orange, with a large hero statement, glass-and-steel imagery and clean industry pages.

Development

Built on WordPress with Albanian and English versions the team maintains itself, supported by ongoing SEO, ads and social media.

Technologies

  • WordPress
  • WPBakery

Result

Fintrade presents its industries, projects and team in two languages on a site its staff manage themselves.

Highlights

  • Industry pages for each sector served
  • Albanian and English content
  • Projects and careers sections
  • Part of the Finman Group family of sites
