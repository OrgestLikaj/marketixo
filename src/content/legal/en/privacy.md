## 2. What we process and why

### Visiting the website

When you open a page, our hosting provider's web server automatically records technical data: IP address, date and time, the page requested, referrer, browser and operating system. We need this to deliver the website and to keep it secure (detecting attacks and abuse).

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Retention:** server logs are deleted or anonymised after a maximum of 30 days, unless needed to investigate a specific security incident.

### Contact form and email

When you send a project request or email us, we process the details you provide — name, email, and optionally company, phone, services, budget, preferred contact method and your message — to answer your request and, where relevant, prepare an offer.

- **Legal basis:** steps prior to entering into a contract at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Retention:** we delete enquiries that do not lead to a project after 12 months. If we work together, data is kept for as long as statutory retention obligations require.
- The form also processes your IP address briefly to prevent spam and abuse (rate limiting). It is stored only as a one-way hash and automatically discarded after one hour.

The form is delivered to our mailbox by email. Nothing you submit is stored in a database on this website.

### Cookies and similar technologies

We only use cookies that are necessary for the site to work, unless you give consent. Details are in our [Cookie Policy]({{cookiePolicyUrl}}).

### Analytics and marketing (only with your consent)

If enabled on this site and only after you consent, we use:

- **Google Analytics 4** (Google Ireland Ltd.) to understand how the website is used, with IP anonymisation.
- **Google Ads conversion measurement / Google Tag Manager** (Google Ireland Ltd.) to measure our campaigns.
- **Meta Pixel** (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) to measure the performance of our Facebook and Instagram ads.

- **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer; this does not affect processing before withdrawal.
- These providers may transfer data to the United States. Google and Meta are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection.

## 3. Recipients

We share personal data only where necessary: with our hosting provider (which processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement), our email provider, and — only with consent — the analytics and advertising providers listed above. We never sell personal data.

## 4. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
- have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16),
- have data erased (Art. 17) or restricted (Art. 18),
- receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20),
- object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21),
- withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise these rights, email {{email}}. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — for example in your country of residence, or in Albania the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDP).

## 5. Security

This website is served exclusively over HTTPS, uses strict security headers and a Content Security Policy, and loads no third-party scripts before consent. Form submissions are validated on the server and protected against spam and abuse.

## 6. Automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
