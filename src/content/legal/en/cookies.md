Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. This website uses as few as possible, and nothing beyond what is strictly necessary until you choose otherwise.

## Your choice

When analytics or marketing tools are active, a banner asks for your consent on your first visit. **Rejecting is as easy as accepting**, and the website works the same either way. You can change your decision at any time with the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of every page or below.

## Necessary cookies

These are required for the website to work and are set without consent.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mx_consent` | Remembers your cookie choices | 6 months |
| `mx_lang` | Remembers the language you selected | 12 months |

## Analytics cookies (only with consent)

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics 4 | Distinguishes visitors for anonymous statistics | 2 years |
| `_ga_<ID>` | Google Analytics 4 | Keeps the session state | 2 years |

## Marketing cookies (only with consent)

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_fbp` | Meta (Facebook, Instagram) | Measures the performance of our ads | 3 months |
| `_gcl_au` | Google Ads | Measures conversions from our ads | 3 months |

Analytics and marketing cookies are only set if the corresponding tool is enabled on this website **and** you have consented. We use Google Consent Mode v2, so Google services also receive your choice.

## Managing cookies in your browser

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking necessary cookies may mean the website forgets your language or consent choice.
